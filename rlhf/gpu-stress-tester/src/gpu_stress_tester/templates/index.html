Training glossary
Round
A complete generation, SFT, and DPO cycle. The next round continues from the previous DPO checkpoint.
Samples / round
The number of ADMEDVOICE audio clips selected for candidate generation in each round.
SFT
Supervised fine-tuning on the known ADMEDVOICE transcripts.
DPO
Direct Preference Optimization on pairs selected by transcript-based word error rate.
Epoch
One complete pass through the current round's training examples.
Batch size
The number of clips processed together on the GPU. Larger batches generally require more VRAM.
Gradient accumulation
Combines gradients from several batches before an optimizer update, increasing the effective batch without matching VRAM growth.
